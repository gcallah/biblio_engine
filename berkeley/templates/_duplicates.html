Possible duplicates

People, entries and publishers that look like one record entered twice. Check the two (their links open their pages), then click Keep this one on the better record: everything that refers to the other (its entries, authors, index terms, reprints and so on) moves to the one kept, any of its blank fields are filled in from the other, and the other is deleted. If they are really different, click Not the same.
