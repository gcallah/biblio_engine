About the Michael Oakeshott Bibliography

This is an open source project written by:

  • Gene Callahan
  • Adnan Bin Hoq
  • Dennis Fenchenko
  • Wenxuan Ma
  • Kyra Thompson

with the aid of Paul Logston.

The Michael Oakeshott Bibliography is a searchable bibliography of works by and about Michael Oakeshott. The engine allows easy imports and exports, and search on multiple fields.
